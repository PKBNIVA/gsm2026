1 · Conclusion

Well built, carefully hedged, and not yet safe or useful to publish

The 162 pages are technically sound and the research behind them is real. But they are written as an account of how the research was done, they contradict each other on prices and facts, they print claims about competitors that the pages themselves call unverified, and they leave out Sociana's own price. They should be fixed in the order below before any more are opened to Google.

0 / 40"Sociana vs" pages that show Sociana's ₹65,000 starting price
1,337links to other sites with no nofollow mark
6 / 162titles that say "India"

What works

  • Every page has one main heading, a unique title and a unique description; no title is over 60 characters.
  • 159 pages carry article markup with a named author and a date; 153 carry FAQ markup.
  • Every page has a comparison table; facts carry a check date.
  • The "Pick X when" lists and "where Sociana is not the right pick" blocks are honest and useful.
  • The alternatives pages open with a real answer.

What holds them back

  • Claims about competitors that could be disputed: discontinued products, ownership, unverified prices, tiny review samples.
  • Facts that disagree within a page or between pages.
  • About 2,000 passages about method, checking and disclosure.
  • Titles and headings in phrases nobody searches.
  • Sociana's price, record and main difference missing or buried.
  • 3,304 links, mostly to competitor homepages, 1,337 passing link value.

8 · Sources and links

Citing without strengthening competitors

Measured across all 162 pages: 3,304 links to 80 other sites inside page content, on 151 pages. 1,967 are marked nofollow and 1,337 are not. The most-linked sites are whova.com (175), cvent.com (152), eventbrite.com (137), swapcard.com (122) and zoho.com (118).

PatternScaleRecommendation
A "source" link on every table row, to the same competitor homepageAbout 2,740 linksRemove. One sources list per page
A bare competitor domain as the link textAbout 300, mostly in "Sources and dates" listsPlain text: "Cvent pricing page (checked 19 Oct 2026)"
A competitor's brand name or domain as the link text on best-of guides, which helps them mostAbout 190Remove the link; keep the name as text
A homepage linked as proof that something is absent52 for cvent.com aloneNo link; a homepage cannot prove absence
Links with no nofollow mark1,337Make nofollow the default for every outside link in page content
Neutral sources (Wikipedia, DataReportal, news sites)FewKeep, as nofollow
Proposed citation rule. Name the source and the check date in plain text, with no link by default. One "Sources and dates" list at the bottom of each page. Link only the exact page behind a price or ownership claim a buyer might dispute, marked nofollow, with link text such as "pricing page, checked 19 Oct 2026". Never use a competitor's name or keyword as link text, and never link a competitor's homepage. Keep a dated screenshot on file for anything not linked.

The trade-off. Nofollow is a hint to search engines, not a guarantee. With fewer links a reader cannot check a claim in one click, so the page's credibility rests on the date and the correction route.

9 · Improvement plan

Five steps, in order

1
Remove or source the disputable claims

Everything in section 2A: discontinued and ownership claims, unverified and derived prices, tiny-sample negatives, accusations. Check: no page contains "we have not verified" beside a price, and every corporate claim has a named source and date.

2
Make the facts agree

One record per competitor (price, platforms, WhatsApp, rupee billing, managed team, ownership, check date), with every page drawing from it. Fix the contradictions and broken text in sections 2B and 2C. Check: a search for each competitor's price returns one value across all pages.

3
Change the page patterns once

Most faults repeat because the pages share patterns: the disclosure, the per-row source link, the "not verified" sentence, the title and heading formulas, the Sociana price cell, the nofollow default. Changing the pattern fixes them on every page. Check: disclosure once per page; one date per table; 0 followed links to other sites; Sociana's from-price on every page that shows a rival's.

4
Rewrite for the buyer and for answers

Per page: answer-first opening, question headings, deciding-rows table, FAQ answers that start with the answer, the standard Sociana description. Check: each page's first 60 words name both products and who each suits.

5
Open in stages, then measure

Open pages in the order below, adding each stage to the sitemap and the AI reference file. Track positions and add ten "alternatives" and "X vs Y" questions to the monthly AI test.
