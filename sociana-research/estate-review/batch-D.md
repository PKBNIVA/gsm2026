Counting rules. Point 1 count = verification/gap statements ("Not verified", "nothing we could confirm", "no detail recorded", "(unverified)") + fixed disclosure/date blocks (header disclosure line, "Not listed means not found that day", "No like-for-like total has been run", Sources-and-dates block, Sources-and-disclosure block; approach pages: Published/Written-by line, Disclosure paragraph, Method line, Sources paragraph) + any "What changed" note. Point 7 anchors counted: competitor brand or bare domain used as link text (the "Sources and dates" lines and "Polling tool prices" style lines); the "(source ...)" links are anchored on the word "source" and are not counted as brand anchors.
Across the 17 "(2026)" pages the same defects recur, so each is listed only briefly where it repeats: (a) no Sociana price, no 300+ events, no own-brand store app, no managed team in any "Where Sociana fits"; (b) "Who should pick which", "Fit at a glance", "At a glance", "Where they differ", "Related" are headings with no searchable words; (c) FAQ "compare quotes on one scenario" / "same attendee count and days" boilerplate even where it makes no sense; (d) "Facts as of 19 Oct 2026" header against sources "checked 23/24 Oct 2026" on several pages.
