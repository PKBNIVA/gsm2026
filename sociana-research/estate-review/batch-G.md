# Batch G review: 20 alternatives pages on sociana.in

Shared template findings (apply to every page below unless the page says otherwise):
- Template founder-facing passages (6 per page): "Checked on <date> against public sources", "Published by Sociana, which sells one of the platforms listed and labels it as ours", "Prices are as published or cited on the date shown ... Ask each vendor for a written quote", "'Not listed' means we could not find it on the vendor's pages that day; it is not a statement that the vendor lacks it", the "Status" line ("pages were live on <date>"), and the Disclosure paragraph ("we correct or reply within 5 working days"). Per-row "checked <date>" repeats on every table row. Counts below are 6 plus page-specific extras.
- Every page except whova omits any Sociana price. The only price quoted anywhere in the batch is on whova ("Launch starts at ₹65,000 per occasion ex GST ... 7-10 days"). 300+ events and the own-brand store app never appear. The Sociana row reads "pricing, checked ..." with no figure.
- Every page except webex has a template meta description ("Alternatives to X compared for Indian events: who each platform fits, with dated sources, and where Sociana differs.") and a title of the form "X alternatives — Sociana": no "vs", "pricing", "India" or year. Heading "Reasons teams pick X" and "In short" carry no searchable words.
- Date mismatch on nearly every page: banner and Disclosure say "19 Oct 2026" (or 23 Oct) while table rows, Status and India paragraph cite 23 or 24 Oct 2026.
- Run-together words such as "In one lineStay", "Published priceEssential:", "Rupee billingNot listed" are very likely text-extraction artefacts of the scrape, not page defects; not counted as typos.
- Link counts: "[LINK→" total includes 1 WhatsApp CTA and 1 bare-domain anchor in the pricing paragraph (e.g. "nunify.com [LINK→...]"). All other competitor links use the anchor "source". So per page: competitor brand-name anchors 0, bare-domain anchors 1.
