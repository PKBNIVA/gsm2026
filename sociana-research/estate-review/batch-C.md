Generic headings with no searchable words on every vendor page: "Who should pick which", "Fit at a glance", "At a glance", "Where they differ", "Related". No title contains "India", "alternatives" or "best"; vendor-page titles carry "vs", "(2026)", "pricing". Point 7: anchors on all vendor pages are the word "source" (no brand anchors) plus 1-2 bare competitor domains in "Sources and dates" and one WhatsApp CTA; internal links are not shown as [LINK→ in the text.
