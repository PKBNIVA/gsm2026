Home / About

Kochi. Since 2009.

Icecube Event Management Pvt. Ltd. plans and produces corporate, government and international events across India, from our office in Kadavanthra, Kochi.

We're at our best when the stakes are high: a Prime Minister's programme on an island cut off from the mainland, a parliamentary committee at the Grand Hyatt, a ministerial address to an open-air crowd at Palakkad Fort Maidan.

The same discipline runs through smaller work too: a team bonding day for Indian Oil, a hybrid seminar in Chennai, a 30-day roadshow through Ernakulam. One cue sheet, technical rehearsals, and a show caller who knows every second of the programme.
